About this tool

Filling a PDF form should not mean printing it, writing by hand, scanning it back, or uploading a private document to a stranger's server. This tool lets you open a PDF, click anywhere to add text or a checkmark, adjust the size to fit the form's lines, and download a clean filled copy. It works on multi-page forms and there is no sign-up, no watermark, and no file upload — the processing runs entirely in your own browser.

Is it really private?

Yes. Unlike most online PDF services, this tool does not upload your file to a server. The PDF is read into your browser's memory on your own computer or phone, processed there, and handed straight back to you as a download. We never receive, see, store, or transmit the document.
