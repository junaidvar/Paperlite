About this tool

Signing a PDF normally means printing it, signing by hand, scanning it back, and emailing a large file — or uploading a contract to a website you have to trust. PaperLite lets you place a signature image directly onto the PDF in your browser. The document and your signature never leave your device. This is well suited to contracts, agreements, letters and forms where the content is private. For the cleanest result, use a signature saved as a transparent PNG (signed on a tablet, or with the background removed) so no white box covers the form.

How to sign a PDF online

  1. Load the PDF you need to sign by clicking the drop area or dragging it in.
  2. Click the image / signature button and choose your signature image (a transparent PNG works best).
  3. Drag the signature onto the signature line; drag its corner to resize it to fit.
  4. Add a date or printed name with the text-box button if the document needs it.
  5. Download the signed PDF and review it before sending.

Is it really private?

Yes. Unlike most online PDF services, this tool does not upload your file to a server. The file is read into your browser's memory on your own computer or phone, processed there, and handed straight back to you as a download. We never receive, see, store, or transmit it. Closing or refreshing the page clears it from memory.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add my signature to a PDF for free?

Load the PDF, click the image button, and choose a picture of your signature. You can drag it onto the signature line and resize it. It is free, with no signup and no upload.

What signature image works best?

A PNG with a transparent background gives the cleanest result because only the ink shows. A plain photo of a signature on white paper will place a white rectangle over that part of the form.

Is my contract uploaded when I sign it?

No. The PDF and the signature are combined inside your own browser and never sent to a server, which matters for private contracts and agreements.

Can I also add the date and my printed name?

Yes. Use the text-box button to type the date, your name, or any other detail, and position each one where the document requires it.

Is an image signature legally valid?

Whether a particular signature is legally accepted depends on the document and the laws and rules that apply to it. This tool places a visible signature image; it does not provide certified or cryptographic e-signatures. Check the requirements for your specific document.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Use Prev and Next to move between pages and add a signature or initials on each page that needs one.
