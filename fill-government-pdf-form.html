About this tool

Government and official forms are exactly the documents you should be most careful uploading anywhere. They contain identity numbers, addresses, financial details and signatures. PaperLite fills them without uploading: the form is opened in your own browser, you place your answers and signature on it, and the finished PDF is saved straight back to your device. We never receive or store it. There is no account to create and no watermark stamped on an official document.

How to fill an official PDF form online

  1. Open this page and load the official PDF form by clicking the drop area or dragging the file in.
  2. Use the text-box button to type your details onto each line of the form.
  3. Use the checkmark button for tick boxes, and the image button to add a scanned or drawn signature.
  4. Move through pages with Prev and Next for multi-page government forms.
  5. Download the completed PDF and check it carefully before submitting it to the authority.

Is it really private?

Yes. Unlike most online PDF services, this tool does not upload your file to a server. The file is read into your browser's memory on your own computer or phone, processed there, and handed straight back to you as a download. We never receive, see, store, or transmit it. Closing or refreshing the page clears it from memory.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to fill a government form online?

With this tool, yes, because the form is never uploaded. It is opened and edited only inside your own browser, so identity and financial details on official forms are never sent to a server.

Do I need to create an account to fill an official form?

No. There is no signup and no email required. This matters for official documents, where you should not have to register with a third party to complete a government form.

Will there be a watermark on my official document?

No. The downloaded PDF is clean, with no watermark or branding, which is essential for forms submitted to a government office or authority.

Can I add my signature to a government form?

Yes. Use the image button to place a scanned or photographed signature, then resize and position it on the signature line. A transparent PNG signature gives the cleanest result.

The official form is a flat scan with no fillable boxes — can I still use it?

Yes. This tool places text anywhere you click, so it works on scanned official forms that have no built-in form fields, which many free tools cannot handle.

Should I check the form before submitting it?

Always. Text is placed where you position it, so open the finished PDF and verify every field is correct and aligned before submitting it to any authority. You are responsible for the final document.
