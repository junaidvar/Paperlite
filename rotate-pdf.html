About this tool

Scans and phone photos often come out rotated. Apply a 90°, 180° or 270° turn to every page and download the fixed PDF — privately, without uploading anything.

How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Load your PDF by clicking the drop area or dragging it in.
  2. Choose a rotation: 90° one way, 180°, or 90° the other way.
  3. The rotation is applied to every page.
  4. The corrected PDF downloads to your device.

Is it really private?

Yes. Unlike most online PDF services, this tool does not upload your file to a server. The file is read into your browser's memory on your own computer or phone, processed there, and handed straight back to you as a download. We never receive, see, store, or transmit it. Closing or refreshing the page clears it from memory.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?

No. Rotation only changes page orientation; the content and quality are unchanged.

Is my file uploaded to rotate it?

No. The rotation is applied in your browser and the document never leaves your device.

Why are my scanned pages sideways?

Scanners and phone cameras often save pages in the wrong orientation. Rotating fixes this so the PDF reads correctly and prints the right way up.

Does the rotation apply to all pages?

Yes. The chosen rotation is applied to every page in the document in one step.

Can I rotate only one page?

This tool rotates the whole document. To rotate a single page, split that page out first, rotate it, then merge it back.
