Why "nothing uploaded" matters

Most online PDF services send your file to their servers to process it. That means your contracts, ID documents, bank statements and forms briefly live on someone else's computer. PaperLite is built differently: every tool runs as code inside your own browser, so the file is read, processed and returned to you without ever being transmitted. We could not see your documents even if we wanted to.

Free to use

All tools are currently free with no account and no watermark. Because the work happens on your device rather than our servers, there is nothing for us to meter — so you can use them as much as you need.
