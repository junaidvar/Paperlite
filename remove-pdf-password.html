About this tool

If you have a password-protected PDF that you can already open but find it inconvenient to enter the password every time, enter the known password here to produce an unlocked copy. This works only when you know the password; it is not a password recovery or bypass tool, and it should only be used on documents you are entitled to access.

Is it really private?

Yes. Unlike most online PDF services, this tool does not upload your file to a server. The PDF is read into your browser's memory on your own computer or phone, processed there, and handed straight back to you as a download. We never receive, see, store, or transmit the document.
