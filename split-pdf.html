About this tool

Choose the page range you want to extract, or split every page into its own file. This is useful for separating one form from a bundle or sharing only the relevant pages of a document — done privately, on your device.

How to split a PDF

  1. Load your PDF by clicking the drop area or dragging it in.
  2. Enter a page range to extract, or choose to split every page into its own file.
  3. Click the split button.
  4. The extracted pages download to your device.

Is it really private?

Yes. Unlike most online PDF services, this tool does not upload your file to a server. The file is read into your browser's memory on your own computer or phone, processed there, and handed straight back to you as a download. We never receive, see, store, or transmit it. Closing or refreshing the page clears it from memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract just one section of a PDF?

Yes. Enter the start and end page of the range you want and only those pages are saved into a new file.

Is my document uploaded to split it?

No. Splitting is done entirely in your browser; the document never leaves your device.

Can I split a PDF into single pages?

Yes. Choose the split-every-page option and every page is saved as its own PDF, delivered together in a single zip file so your browser doesn't block multiple downloads.

Will splitting change the page quality?

No. Pages are copied unchanged, so quality is identical to the original.

What if my PDF is password protected?

Unlock it first with the Remove Password tool, only for documents you are entitled to open, then split the unlocked copy.
